How it would work

The build

  • When. Once a day at 7:00 AM on the Mini, plus on demand from Regenerate (button, or "Regenerate today" in ⌘K).
  • Input. Every Needs you conversation, plus Waiting ones whose last message is under 7 days old. Roughly the last 30 messages of each, with the contact's CRM priority, tags and notes. About 80 threads and 100k tokens on a normal day.
  • Model. One Opus 5.5 call through the CLIProxyAPI gateway the server already uses for titles. It returns a short brief, up to 6 groups (title plus one line of context), and up to 50 ranked conversations, each with a one-line reason.
  • Storage. One row per day in a new Convex table, comma_today_queue: builtAt, brief, groups[], items[{conversationId, rank, group, reason}], candidateCount.
  • Live. The client joins that row to the live chat summaries. Settling, replying and reading update the lens the way they do today; only the order and the groups come from the build.
  • New since. A conversation that turns Needs you after builtAt and is not in the queue goes to the top group, newest first. Regenerate folds it into the ranked groups.

Calls this mock makes, each one a recommendation

  1. Where it lives. A new first tab, Today, that the app opens to. The other option is a "Prioritize" sort inside Needs you, which keeps the tab row at six but hides the groups.
  2. A ranked thread gets a new message. It stays in its slot and shows the unread dot. Moving it to New would reshuffle the plan every time someone double-texts.
  3. A reply leaves the queue. Sending a message to a Today thread settles it from Today, and a Waiting thread drops out when they answer. Rank numbers keep their gaps, so you can see what's done.
  4. Below the line. The Needs you threads left out stay one tap away in a folded group at the bottom, never hidden.
  5. Regenerate. Rebuilds the whole queue from what's open now. The day's settled count carries over, so progress never resets.
  6. Out of scope here: the phone layout (same list, the header becomes the first row) and per-group bulk settle.
